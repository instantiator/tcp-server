We're currently using Keycloak as the 'bundled' OIDC service. Although it works nicely, it's quite heavy, and consumes a lot of resources. This has contributed to long start-up times and degraded system performance while it is running.

Zitadel is posed as an alternative to Keycloak, and early exploration suggests it might be less resource-intensive.

Please explore what it would take to switch away from Keycloak. Consider Zitadel, and consider other alternatives that could stand-in.

Please make a recommendation between these options:

1. continue with Keycloak
2. switch to Zitadel
3. switch to another service

Criteria to consider:

- Lightweight (Keycloak to beat!)
- Containerised (easy to spin up as the 'bundled' option)
- Free and OSS, with a permissive license
- Compatible (supports OIDC / OAuth 2 standards, no code changes required to interact with it, ideally no special cases[^1])
- Well-supported, well-maintained, well-documented
- Easy to configure (some of our launch scripts need to ensure that the realm and test user are created)
- Low effort to switch
- Could share a database container - ie. we currently use postgres for other aspects of our work, and it would be good if the new service didn't need to run its own database (this is a nice to have, though)

[^1]: I'm aware that Keycloak has some special cases (although I don't recall the details) - again, Keycloak to beat!

Make a recommendation and explain why your recommendation is stronger than the other options. Please draft a new ADR in `docs/ADRs/` describing the context, options, recommendation, and potential impact of the proposed change (if any).
