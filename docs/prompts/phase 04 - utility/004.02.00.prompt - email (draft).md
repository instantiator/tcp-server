> | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
> | [Email](usage.gaps.md#email) | No way to read or send email | An IMAP/SMTP MCP service; sending always goes through approval |
>
> ## Email
>
> **Today:** none.
>
> **Recommend:**
>
> - An email MCP service in the catalogue, configured with IMAP/SMTP host, port
>   and a credential secret. Add Gmail/Outlook OAuth later, as their app-password
>   routes keep narrowing.
> - Tools: `list_unread`, `read_message`, `draft_reply`, `send` (with approval),
>   and `mark_handled`.
> - Split read and send across roles (tool allow-lists). Sending is always an
>   [approved action](usage.gaps.md#action-approval) until the user opts a template reply into
>   auto-send.
> - Every inbound message is [untrusted content](usage.gaps.md#untrusted-inbound-content).
> - Record handled message IDs so a recurring check doesn't process the same
>   email twice (see [duplicate actions](usage.gaps.md#duplicate-actions)).
