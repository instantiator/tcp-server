## Notes for converting these pages

- Several GOV.UK pages are guides split into parts. Fetch the whole guide from its print view by adding `/print` to the URL (for example `https://www.gov.uk/self-employed-records/print`). This applies to: online and distance selling, marketing and advertising, business records, cash basis, Self Assessment tax returns and become a sole trader.
- Keep the text only. Leave out logos, crests, images and page navigation.
- The allergen guidance ends with a "© Crown copyright" block that names the Open Government Licence. Keep the licence line; the rest can go.

## Attribution

Each knowledge file carries this attribution in its front matter, and `LICENSES.md` repeats it:

> Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

The licence is at <https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/>. The Health and Safety Executive asks for "Contains public sector information published by the Health and Safety Executive and licensed under the Open Government Licence", so use that wording for `hazard-labels.md`.

## Gaps

- **Candles and wax melts.** No open-licence page gives a candle maker's full labelling checklist. The HSE page explains hazard labels in general. The regulations adviser should say when a candle question goes beyond it.
- **Postage prices.** Not knowledge on purpose: they change too often. Keep them in a `postage` records table.
- **Review dates.** These pages change. 005.02 adds review-by dates to knowledge packs; until then, check the pages again before relying on them.
