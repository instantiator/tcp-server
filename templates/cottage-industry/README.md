# Cottage industry

A company that helps one person in the UK run a small home business as a second income. It suits crafts, baked goods, candles, soap and other small-batch products sold online and at markets.

The agents draft and prepare. The owner approves anything sent to a customer, every promise (discounts, refunds, delivery dates) and all spending.

There is no model in this template. Choose one when you import it.

## Roles

- **Planner** plans each task and gives its steps to the roles below.
- **Enquiry handler** sorts customer messages and drafts replies.
- **Order manager** records orders, tracks them, works out postage and prepares packing lists.
- **Promises reviewer** checks drafts for discounts, refunds, delivery dates and other promises the owner hasn't agreed.
- **Bookkeeper** keeps income and expenses, prepares tax-year summaries and drafts invoices.
- **Materials buyer** watches materials stock and drafts supplier orders.
- **Listing writer** writes and refreshes product listings.
- **Regulations adviser** answers questions about UK rules, only from its knowledge documents.

The company turns on the **records** service (orders, products, materials, income and expenses) and the **utilities** service (exact sums, dates and unit conversion).

## Not legal or tax advice

Nothing this company produces is legal, tax or financial advice. Its knowledge is general UK guidance, mostly from GOV.UK, and it may be out of date or not fit your situation. Check the current official guidance, or ask a qualified adviser, for anything that matters.
